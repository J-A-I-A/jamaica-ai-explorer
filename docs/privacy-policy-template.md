## Who we are

## The short version

- We do not ask for your email address, phone number, or any other contact
  details. There is nowhere on this site to enter them.
- Giving your name is optional unless you are responding for an organisation.
- We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not track you across
  other websites.
- Questions you ask the Smart Search assistant are sent to a third party to
  generate an answer, and are not stored by us.

## What we collect, and when

### When you send feedback

The feedback form is the only place on this site where information you type is
stored. If you complete it, we store:

- **Your response type** — whether you are answering as an individual or on
  behalf of an organisation.
- **The organisation's name**, if you are responding for one. This is required
  for organisational responses, because a submission made on behalf of a body
  needs to say which body.
- **Your name**, if you choose to give it. This is always optional. Leave it
  blank to respond anonymously.
- **A few optional details about you** — age range, employment status, and how
  familiar you are with A.I. for individuals; organisation type and industry
  sector for organisations. Every one of these can be left unanswered.
- **Your ratings** of individual recommendations, including the ones you chose
  to skip, and any comment you attached to a rating.
- **Your general comments**, together with the topics you filed them under.
- **The stage the policy was at** when you responded, and the date and time of
  the submission.

We do not store your IP address, your browser, or your device alongside your
submission.

### When you use Smart Search

Smart Search sends the question you type, and the conversation so far, to
[MODEL PROVIDER] so that an answer can be generated. We do not write those
questions to our database, and the conversation is held only in your browser —
closing or refreshing the page discards it.

Please do not type personal or sensitive information into Smart Search. Once a
question has been sent for an answer, we cannot recall it.

### Information handled automatically, and not kept

- **Your IP address** is used, in memory only, to limit how many feedback
  submissions and assistant questions can come from one address in a given
  period. It is not written to our database and it is discarded when the
  application restarts.
- **reCAPTCHA.** The feedback form uses Google reCAPTCHA to tell people from
  automated scripts. Google receives your IP address and information about your
  interaction with the page, and handles it under
  [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We receive
  only Google's pass-or-fail answer.
- **Server logs** may record ordinary request information for a short period as
  part of running the service. [CONFIRM OR AMEND: describe your hosting
  provider's log retention.]

### Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies.

Your choice of light or dark theme is saved in your browser's local storage so
the site remembers it on your next visit. It never leaves your device and we
cannot read it. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

reCAPTCHA sets its own cookies on the feedback page. These are Google's, and are
covered by Google's privacy policy linked above.

## Why we use it, and on what basis

Feedback is collected so that the National A.I. Task Force and the Government of
Jamaica can take public views into account when finalising the national A.I.
policy. Responses may be summarised, quoted, or reported in aggregate as part of
that process.

If you give your name or your organisation's name, it may appear in a published
summary of the consultation. **If you would rather not be identified, leave the
name fields blank** — the form works without them.

## Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2020 you have the right to ask us:

- what personal data we hold about you,
- for a copy of it,
- to correct it if it is wrong,
- to delete it, or
- to stop using it in a particular way.

Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will respond within the time the Act allows.

Please note: if you submitted feedback without giving your name, we have no way
to identify which response was yours, and so no way to retrieve or delete it. To
be able to exercise these rights over a submission, give your name when you
send it.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you may complain
to the Office of the Information Commissioner of Jamaica.
